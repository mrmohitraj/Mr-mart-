ℹ️ About Mr Fasal Wala

Connecting farmers directly to buyers, nationwide

Mr Fasal Wala is India's dedicated online marketplace built exclusively for crops and crop waste. We connect farmers, agri-traders and industrial buyers directly — removing unnecessary middlemen and helping both sides get a fairer deal.

Our Mission

To digitize India's crop and crop-residue trade so that every farmer, however small, can reach verified buyers across the country and get real-time, transparent mandi pricing.

What We Offer

  • A curated marketplace for 17+ crop categories and 14+ crop waste categories.
  • Verified seller badges and district/state-level delivery estimates.
  • Daily market price tracking to help you time your sale or purchase.
  • Simple listing tools so any farmer can start selling in minutes.

Why Crop Waste Matters

Millions of tonnes of paddy husk, wheat straw, bagasse and other residues are burned or wasted every year. Mr Fasal Wala creates a direct channel to biomass, fodder and industrial buyers — turning waste into an additional income stream for farmers.
